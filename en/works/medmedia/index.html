MedMedia

student media centre

branding · social media · motion design

AI-assisted description

Written from project materials. An author-written description will follow.

Media for a new generation

MedMedia is a complete redesign of a student medical media identity, introduced in March 2026. The main audience is young people, especially students. The system needs to work across information posts, entertainment, offline events and other formats. Rather than one fixed layout, it provides a shared language that supports different subjects and compositions.

Expression with a clear structure

A purple accent, expressive mark and contrasting type create an energetic character. Dark surfaces separate content from graphics, while bright colour fields support more emotional communication. The same elements work in a short announcement or a denser piece. Recognition comes from shared visual rules, not identical compositions.

A shared system for the team

Before the redesign, most visual content was produced by one designer. After the update, the team grew to three and produced more design content. Shared rules allow different contributors to maintain one recognisable language. The system stays flexible enough for photography, illustration, text-led posts and event materials.

Results and context

From September 2025 to September 2026, the platform’s total reach grew by about 13.5 times and likes increased by roughly 65%. These figures describe the media platform’s overall development and cannot be attributed solely to the redesign.

Design content and photography

One post using the new design system received around 10,000 reach, 19,700 views and 111 likes. Design content is published regularly and independently attracts meaningful reach and reactions. Photography remains an important part of MedMedia and often performs better because the audience enjoys seeing themselves, other students, faculty and events. Platform results therefore need to be considered across different content types.

1 September: the system in use

The new visual system was used in materials and audience touchpoints on 1 September. Acquisition methods were similar in 2025 and 2026: offline interaction, invitations to follow, gifts and a photo zone. Subscriber growth provides context for the system’s use; it does not establish that design alone caused the increase.
