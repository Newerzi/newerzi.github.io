March — November 2026 · art direction · identity · social media

медмедиа

A new visual language for a student media centre.

A redesign and an evolving system that connects publications, the team and event spaces.

01

More than a visual refresh

Goal 01

Renew the image

Keep continuity with the existing media centre while making its visual language brighter and more relevant to a young audience.

Goal 02

Create a team system

Build flexible principles for publications, merchandise and events that can be used by several designers.

02

Continuity without inertia

Two anchors remained: the signature purple and the original symbol. Colour grew more vivid, forms more precise and compositions more energetic.

The goal was not identical templates, but recognition that leaves room for different formats and editorial moods.

03

Controlled chaos

Bright accents, expressive typography and dense compositions form a flexible set of principles. Every element supports the message rather than merely filling space.

04

One system, multiple authors

The language supports recurring publications, covers, photography and information design. Tasks are distributed by complexity and experience while art direction holds the overall direction.
