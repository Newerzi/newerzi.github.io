Astra VPS

From a name to a digital service.

A visual identity and website designed for a new game-hosting service.

01

Two goals. One launch.

Primary goal

Build the brand

The product began with a name and a game-hosting service. Launch required a mark, a colour system and a consistent visual language.

Additional goal

Create a digital platform

The website complemented the Telegram bot, presenting the offer and supporting selection, purchase and service management.

02

A form rooted in the product

The mark began with a cloud, a direct association with digital infrastructure. Its geometry also evokes a star and an aster flower.

A dark palette and blue accents carry the technological mood from identity into the interface.

03

From first view to server management

The site follows a clear sequence, helping people understand the offer and move forward without unnecessary distractions.

01Discover
02Configure
03Purchase
04Manage
04

One language across touchpoints

The same rules continue through the dashboard, components and support. Type, colour and accents keep the service recognisable across sections.

Astra VPS gained a recognisable identity and a dedicated platform for discovery, ordering and ongoing management.
