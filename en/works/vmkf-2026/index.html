Written from project materials. An author-written description will follow.

A system for the forum

ARMCF is a real project for a forum’s visual identity. Its audience brings together an established professional community and younger participants. The design needs to feel credible and clear while staying bright and contemporary. Simple memorable patterns, saturated colours and clear graphics form the foundation. The identity relates to the forum’s organisers, including ASO Russia.

Scaling across applications

One visual language extends to publications, badges, print, banners, navigation, merchandise and participant materials. Large applications use colour fields and patterns; smaller formats prioritise clear text and a recognisable mark. The photographs show the design in the forum’s real setting. The identity retains its character alongside people, architecture and information at different scales.
