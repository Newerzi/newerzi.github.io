Wexon Network

business messenger

web design

AI-assisted description

Written from project materials. An author-written description will follow.

Conversations as a workspace

Wexon Network is a modern messenger concept. Its product idea is to reduce the gap between conversations, tasks and the tools people use throughout the working day. The concept combines chat sorting and pinning, task assignment, widgets and AI features. It is an exploration of a product and its visual language, not a launched service.

A digital visual language

The visual direction draws on technology, financial interfaces, crypto and P2P culture. Dark surfaces and a vivid green accent create a focused digital character. A compact mark connects the interface, app icon and communication. It acts as a shared element rather than a separate decoration.

Fewer switches between tools

In this concept, a chat is a place to organise work as well as discuss it. Pinned conversations keep important context close, tasks connect to the discussion, and widgets provide quick access to useful information. AI features belong within that workspace. Their role is to support work in a familiar interface rather than introduce another separate service.
